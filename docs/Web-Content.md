### Scan to BIM — Turning Existing Buildings into Digital Assets

Many existing buildings have outdated, incomplete, or inaccurate documentation. During renovation or refurbishment projects, unreliable drawings often lead to design errors, site rework, and unnecessary costs.

Scancrew bridges the gap between the physical building and digital construction.

We capture the building using 3D laser scanning technology and convert the resulting point cloud into a structured BIM model that becomes a reliable foundation for planning, design, and construction.

### How Scan to BIM Works

1. **Capture the Building** — Existing buildings are scanned using high-precision 3D laser scanners.
2. **Create the Point Cloud** — Individual scans are registered and combined into a complete digital point cloud.
3. **Build the BIM Model** — Our BIM specialists create intelligent building models based on the captured point cloud.
4. **Deliver Your Project Files** — Depending on project requirements, we provide:
   - BIM Models
   - IFC Files
   - CAD Drawings
   - As-Built Documentation

### Ideal Applications

Scan to BIM is ideal for:

- Architectural Planning

- Building Digitization
- BIM-Based Project Delivery

Our BIM models can be developed according to project-specific modeling requirements, classifications, and Levels of Geometry (LOG) and Levels of Information (LOI).

### From Point Cloud to Intelligent Building Model

A point cloud contains millions of measured points — but it is not yet a usable BIM model.

Our BIM specialists convert these measurements into intelligent building elements.

Walls, floors, roofs, windows, doors, stairs, and other building components are modeled according to your project requirements.

### Modeling Scope

Depending on project requirements, BIM models may include:

- Site & Terrain
- Structural Walls
- Columns
- Floors & Slabs
- Roofs
- Doors
- Windows
- Stairs
- Railings
- Rooms & Spaces
- Selected MEP Components

### LOG & LOI Based Modeling

Every project requires a different level of detail.

Instead of using a fixed Level of Detail, we define the required Level of Geometry (LOG) and Level of Information (LOI) according to the project's intended use.

This ensures efficient modeling without unnecessary work.

### Structured BIM Models

A high-quality BIM model is more than accurate geometry.

We create models suitable for:

- Construction Documentation
- IFC Exchange
- Quantity Takeoff
- Coordination
- Future Design Development
- Asset Information

### Quality Assurance

Every BIM model passes through a structured quality control process:

1. BIM Modeling
2. Geometric Verification
3. Model Structure Validation
4. Quality Review
5. Final Delivery
