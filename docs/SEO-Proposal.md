As per the analysis above, we could conclude that the core service vocabulary in Switzerland is structurally low in volume — the majority of commercial terms sit between 10 and 40 searches per month. There is no single keyword in this category capable of carrying a traffic-led strategy.

However, the CPC values tell the more important story. Advertisers are paying CHF 1.60 to CHF 3.90 per click on keywords that receive twenty searches a month, and competitive density on several terms reaches 0.80 to 1.00. Commercial buyers do not bid at those levels on low-volume terms unless the value per acquired client is substantial. This is a low-volume, high-value niche and the campaign is scoped accordingly: the objective is to own positions 1 to 3 across the entire commercial set, not to chase impressions.

It should also be noted that keyword difficulty data is unavailable for the majority of these terms. This is a consequence of low query volume, not an indication that the terms are easy. Ranking positions in this category are held by established Swiss surveying and planning firms with mature domains, and difficulty should be assumed to be moderate.

## 1.1 Starting Position

Scancrew is entering the market without an existing domain. This is a materially different starting position from an established website and it changes both the sequencing and the expected timeline of the campaign. It is addressed directly here because it is the primary constraint on how quickly results can be delivered.

### What a new domain means in practice

- **No historical trust signals.** A newly registered domain carries no link equity, no crawl history and no established topical association. Google will index it, but initial ranking positions for commercial terms will be weak regardless of on-page quality.
- **No existing backlink profile.** Established Swiss competitors in the surveying and BIM space typically hold between 300 and 900 referring domains accumulated over many years. Scancrew begins at zero.
- **No keyword history in Search Console.** There is no impression or query data to baseline against, which means the first three months of the campaign also function as the data-gathering period.
- **Full technical control.** This is the significant advantage. Architecture, URL structure, internal linking, schema and page speed can all be specified correctly from launch rather than retrofitted, which removes an entire category of remedial work.

> **Consultant Insight**
>
> The absence of a domain is not a disadvantage to be corrected — it is the reason the off-page component of this campaign carries more weight than it would for an established site. On-page optimisation alone will not produce commercial rankings for a domain with no authority, however well executed. The campaign is therefore structured so that technical and on-page work is completed in month one, and sustained German-language link acquisition begins from month two and continues without interruption for the duration of the engagement.
>
> Realistically, a new domain in a low-competition, low-volume vertical of this type should expect early informational rankings from month three to four, and commercial term movement from month five onward. The package tiers below differ principally in how much link velocity is applied, which is what compresses that timeline.

## 1.3 Proposed Solution

In response to the market conditions identified in section 1.0 and the new-domain starting position set out in section 1.1, we propose a six month (minimum) On-Page / Off-Page SEO campaign that encompasses technical foundation, SEO-friendly content development and keyword-research-driven meta data optimisation across the website's landing pages.

The campaign runs full circle. Month one establishes the technical and on-page foundation across the site architecture. From month two onward, off-page activity begins and runs continuously, building the German-language link profile required to move the commercial keyword set. These activities are intended to index and rank the website for keywords relevant to the Scancrew service offering in the Swiss German market.

### Campaign Sequence

| Phase | Activity |
|---|---|
| **Month 1** | Technical foundation and full on-page optimisation. Site architecture, URL structure, keyword-to-page mapping, meta data, schema markup, internal linking, indexation setup. No link building in this month — the site must be structurally sound before authority is directed at it. |
| **Month 2 onward** | Off-page campaign begins and runs continuously. German-language guest article placement on DA 30+ blogs, directory and citation building, ongoing content optimisation. On-page refinements continue in parallel as Search Console data becomes available. |
| **Month 3 onward** | First Search Console data available. Keyword targeting is re-baselined against real impression data and the focus keyword set is refined where the data justifies it. |
| **Ongoing** | Monthly keyword ranking report, monthly backlink report, and continuous link acquisition at the velocity defined by the selected package tier. |

## 1.5 Prerequisites

- **Domain registration and hosting.** A .ch domain is strongly recommended for the Swiss market and should be secured as early as possible so that domain age begins accruing.
- **Website development.** Site build to be handled by the client or their developer. The consultant will supply the architecture, URL structure and keyword mapping specification prior to build.
- **Back-end access** for meta data implementation.
- **Google Tag Manager** access.
- **Google Analytics** access.
- **Google Search Console** access, with property verification completed at launch.
- **Google Business Profile** access, or authorisation to create and verify one. This carries significant weight for a Swiss service business and requires a verifiable physical address.
- **Website copy.** Text SEO optimisation will be handled by the consultant. Any additional content required beyond optimisation of existing copy is to be handled by the client.
- **Approval turnaround on outreach articles.** Placements are time-sensitive and delayed approval extends the campaign timeline.
